What's inside
  1. i

    Drawn live, never looped

    Every frame is rendered in real time from the sound, so nothing is pre-made or repeated. The same song never looks quite the same twice.

  2. ii

    It hears the whole song

    The audio is split into frequency bands with beat and tempo detection on top. Bass swells the geometry, the melody turns it, and the high end makes it shimmer, so the pattern follows the shape of a track instead of just pulsing along.

  3. iii

    Your music, or the room

    On Android it can visualize whatever your phone is playing, from Spotify to YouTube. Or switch to the microphone and point it at a singing bowl, a guitar, or a room full of people.

  4. iv

    Swipe, hold, tap

    Swipe to move between patterns. Press and hold for a carousel of all ten. Tap once and the controls step aside so it's only the geometry on screen.

  5. v

    Make it yours

    Tune how hard each frequency band pushes the pattern, then set rotation speed, line thickness, opacity and detail, with stars and cosmic dust drifting behind. Start from presets like Bass Heavy, Harmonic Focus, Cosmic or Minimal, and save your own.

  6. vi

    Record it

    Capture the visualizer as a video with the sound included, ready to share or keep. On Android, pick 30 or 60 fps and record from the mic or straight from device audio.

  7. vii

    Put it on the big screen

    Send it to the TV with Chromecast on Android or AirPlay on Apple devices. Let it run through a meditation session, a long record, or a party.

  8. viii

    A living wallpaper Android

    Set any pattern as your home screen wallpaper, still listening and still moving to whatever you play.

  9. ix

    A real music player underneath Apple

    Bring in your own tracks and Apple Music playlists, then play them through a ten-band EQ with gapless playback, crossfade, volume leveling, a sleep timer and lock screen controls.
